## 1. The question

Which Areas in Lagos are prone to flooding? FOCUS ON OJO LGA

## 2. Why this question

Lagos as a whole has been plagued by the issues of poor drainage, which leads to flooding in areas of Lagos.
This will help create safe drainage paths during flooding.

## 3. Study area

Lagos State, Ojo LGA.

## 4. What I mean by the terms

prone: places that have a high risk of flooding based on one criterion.

An interactive Map that updates itself based on the rain density report and devises the best drainage routes for each area.
